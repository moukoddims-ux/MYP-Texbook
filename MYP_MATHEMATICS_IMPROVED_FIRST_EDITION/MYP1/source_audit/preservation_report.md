# Preservation report — first edition to improved first edition

**Scope of this note.** It records what the first edition taught, where each topic is taught in this
edition, and what (if anything) was added, merged or left out. The improved edition is a full rewrite:
no sentence of the first edition is reproduced. *Preservation* here means coverage of topics,
mathematical scope, and the Year 1 level of demand.

Source: *MYP Mathematics 1*, first edition, 240 pages, five parts, ten chapters, 45 numbered sections,
back matter (answers, formula bank, glossary, index, references).

## 4. What is deliberately different

- **Wording.** Everything is newly written; the first edition's text is not copied.
- **Examples and exercises.** All 1,224 items and 165 worked examples are new.
- **Figures.** All 48 drawings are new vector art; the first edition had no vector artwork of this kind.
- **Order within a chapter.** Split sections (for example 4.5 became 4.4 and 4.5) change the local
  order but keep the topic sequence.
- **Assessment framing.** Criteria, strands, ATL skills and approaches to learning are named
  explicitly, which the first edition did not do consistently.
